Fall Seasonal Events:
Seliana Fun Fright Fest & Astera Autumn Harvest Fest!

The Astera Autumn Harvest Fest is joined by the new Seliana Fun Fright Fest!
Visit both Gathering Hubs to experience twice the amount of seasonal celebration!

Availability
5 p.m. Thursday, October 15, 2020 — 3:59 p.m. Thursday, November 5, 2020 (Pacific Time)

Seasonal Event Activities

  • Layered armor!
    Complete limited-time event quests for the materials to craft special new equipment, such as the Demonlord α+ armor set, Kadachi Scarf layered armor, and more!
  • Increased login bonuses!
    Lucky Vouchers increased to 2 per day, and login bonuses also include Autumn Harvest Tickets and Fun Fright Tickets!
  • Daily limited bounties!
    Complete limited bounties to get Autumn Harvest Tickets, Harvest Fireworks, Fun Fright Tickets, and Snowmen!
  • Discounts at base facilities!
  • New Handler outfits available for a limited time!
  • New Poogie clothing as well as Guild Card titles, backgrounds, and poses!
  • Nearly all event quests available to play!
    Almost all previous event quests will be made available for the duration of the event.
