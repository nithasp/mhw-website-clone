Collaboration Quest Round 2

Event Quest: "The Survivor"

Conditions: MR 24 or higher
Locale: Hoarfrost Reach
Objective: Hunt a Stygian Zinogre

Completion Rewards

- Materials necessary for forging the Shield-Weaver+ full armor set and upgrading to the Adept Stormslinger light bowgun.

- Materials for creating the new Frostclaw α+ Palico full armor set and the Forgefire α+ Palico weapon.

Event Quest: "Firebreak"

Conditions: MR 24 or higher
Locale: Elder's Recess
Objective: Hunt a Savage Deviljho

- Available after completing the event quest "The Survivor".

Completion Rewards

- Materials to forge the "Banuk α+" armor, which is modeled after the armor that members of the Banuk tribe wear in Horizon Zero Dawn. The Banuk α+ armor has 5 distinct parts, so it can be mixed and matched with any other armor of your choice!

- Daemon Ticket: Necessary for forging the Monster Hunter: World collaboration hunter weapon and Palico weapon and full armor set and for upgrading them to stronger, master rank versions!

Materials necessary to forge special collaboration pendants:

- Focus Type A, Focus Type B, Focus Type C
