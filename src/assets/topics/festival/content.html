Celebrate Monster Hunter World: Iceborne's First Anniversary with a Lineup of Seasonal Events!

Monster Hunter World: Iceborne marks its first anniversary on September 6, 2020.
To celebrate, we're featuring a lineup of past seasonal events, available from August 20 to September 30!
Almost all previous event quests will be made available for the duration of the event. You'll also get more login bonuses, discounts at in-game facilities, daily limited bounties, and more!

Note:

- Some content is only available to players who have purchased the Monster Hunter World: Iceborne expansion.
