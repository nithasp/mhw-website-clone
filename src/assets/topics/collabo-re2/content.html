Event Quest — RE: Return of the Bioweapon

Obtain S.T.A.R.S. Badges to look like Leon or Claire! Create the unique full armor sets "Leon α+" and "Claire α+" and more!

Conditions: MR 20 or higher
Locale: Rotten Vale
Objective: Slay a Blackveil Vaal Hazak

Features of Event Quest — RE: Return of the Bioweapon

Unique to this event quest, effluvium-tainted attacks will not result in effluvial buildup but instead will result in a new "zombified" status effect.
(The skill Effluvia Resistance has no effect during this quest.)

You can cure yourself of the zombified status by making use of "green herbs", a special quest items that you can gather during the quest. And if you find the rarer "red herb", you can combine them with a green herb to craft an item with an even stronger effect. Collect as much as you can of both to prepare yourself against zombification!

Zombified Status

The zombified status effect limits your mobility and nullifies regular healing effects, but it also makes you recover health automatically! Keep in mind that this health recovery is slower when your weapon is drawn.

Raccoon City Collaboration — Rewards

Gestures Raccoon City: Zombify

This gesture allows you to "zombify" yourself at the drop of a hat! Unlike other gestures, it puts you into a "zombified" gesture state that allows you to move around freely. However, zombification from this gesture does not carry the benefits of the actual status effect, such as damage reaction nullification and automatic health recovery. Once you've used the "Raccoon City: Zombify" gesture once, its name will be replaced with "Raccoon City: Un-zombify", which will turn you back to normal.
(Performing a dodge roll while out on a quest will also turn you back to normal.)

Gestures Raccoon City: Zombie Bite

This gesture makes your character lunge forward to bite like a zombie. If some unlucky monster happens to be in the way of your hungry maw, it'll even take some (slight) damage! You can perform this gesture at any time, but it loses some strength if you're not zombified.

Full Armor Sets (2 Types)

Complete the event quest to obtain S.T.A.R.S. Badges, which will allow you to create the full armor sets "Leon α+" and "Claire α+". These armor sets also feature the "Zombie & Effluvia Res" skill, which negates zombification and effluvial buildup!

Pendants (3 Types)

- R.P.D. — A pendant modeled after the Raccoon City Police badge!

- Umbrella — A pendant modeled after the Umbrella Corporation's logo!

- The Tofu Survivor — A pendant that talks when you draw your weapon in certain situations!

Room Decor (11 Types)

Obtain special Raccoon City Collaboration room decor by completing the event quest! Use them to decorate your Seliana room into a slightly more "evil" residence!
You'll also be able to obtain two Raccoon City Collaboration BGM songs for the music player!

There are also some special room decor that you can get by completing delivery requests at the Resource Center, so try the quest multiple times to complete these deliveries!

- Typewriter — Also allows you to save your game!

- Item Box — Can also be used to manage your items!
